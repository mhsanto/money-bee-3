Our Journey

Our story began with years of experience in Hong Kong's lending industry, where we witnessed how advanced credit tools and advisory services reduced debt stress. When we looked at Malaysia, we saw the same challenges high household debt, rising youth bankruptcies, and borrowers confused by too many loan products.

2024

Conceptualised after studying Malaysia's BNPL surge and household debt concerns.

2025

Launched platform with Personal Loan and Debt Consolidation as flagship products.

2025

Expanded to SME Loans, Property Loans, Car Loans, and Islamic Financing.

Future

Our Mission

At Moneybee Sdn. Bhd., our mission is to make borrowing in Malaysia safe, transparent, and empowering. We believe financial products should not trap people in stress but help them move forward whether it's buying a home, consolidating debts, funding education, or growing a business.

We use a hybrid model combining AI technology with human judgment to ensure borrowers are not unfairly rejected by rigid scoring systems. Every Malaysian deserves a fair chance, and our mission is to bridge that gap by offering loans that are accessible, responsible, and supported with continuous guidance.

Our Vision

We envision a future where:

  • Malaysians view borrowing not as a burden, but as a tool for financial growth.
  • Lenders reduce default risks because borrowers are supported before problems escalate.
  • Families, SMEs, and youth can achieve their goals with confidence, knowing they have both technology and people working alongside them.

In the long run, our vision is to become:

  • The most trusted loan-matching platform in Malaysia.
  • A regional leader in ASEAN, expanding our hybrid model to other countries.

Service Providers

We partner with a broad network of trusted financial institutions in Malaysia, ensuring that you have real choices to compare

Fairness, Always

AI helps us find matches fast, but we never let machines decide your future alone. Our human advisors step in to review cases where context matters for example, self-employed workers, gig economy earners, or SMEs with seasonal income.

Transparency & Safety

We only work with licensed banks and financial institutions regulated by Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM). You will never face hidden charges or misleading offers. Every loan offer comes with clear terms tenure, rates, monthly instalments, and conditions.

Post-Loan Support with Case Managers

Most lenders stop caring once the loan is disbursed. We do the opposite. A Case Manager is assigned to you after disbursement to: Monitor repayment progress. Detect early warning signs if you face difficulty. Provide advice before small problems turn into defaults. This is a new standard in Malaysia's loan market ensuring long-term borrower health and lower default rates for lenders.

Financial Education for All

We don't just give you a loan; we teach you how to manage it wisely. Our platform offers blogs, guides, and tools to help you improve financial literacy from BNPL awareness to SME financing tips. Our goal is to reduce Malaysia's household debt stress by building smarter, more confident borrowers.

Our Values

Our values guide every decision we make. They are not just words they are the standards by which we measure ourselves.

1. Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency, and compliance. No shortcuts, no scams, no unlicensed lenders.

2. Responsibility

We encourage borrowing that fits your needs and your capacity, not reckless debt. We advocate responsible lending to lenders focusing on sustainability, not just profit.

3. Innovation with Purpose

We use AI not for gimmicks, but for real impact: faster loan matching, smarter insights, better early detection of risks. Our hybrid AI + Human approach ensures fairness and inclusivity.

4. Human Care

We prioritize human connections. Our team is trained to listen, understand, and guide you through financial decisions with empathy, ensuring you feel supported every step of the way.

5. Inclusivity

We believe everyone deserves access to fair financial services, regardless of background, income level, or past credit history. Our platform is designed to be inclusive, breaking down barriers to financial inclusion.

6. Respect for Faith & Diversity

7. Long-Term Partnership

We build lasting relationships, not just transactions. Our goal is to support you throughout your financial journey, from initial borrowing to long-term financial health.
